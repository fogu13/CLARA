# Impressum (§ 5 DDG)

_Purpose: the legally mandated provider identification ("Anbieterkennzeichnung") for CLARA's website, satisfying § 5 Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG) and the related German media/consumer information duties for a solo business._

> **DRAFT — have a German Fachanwalt (IT-Recht/Datenschutz) review before use.** This template encodes the current statute references (verified July 2026) but must be reviewed and adapted to the founder's actual status (Freiberufler vs. Gewerbe, insurance, registrations) before publication. A missing or wrong Impressum is an Ordnungswidrigkeit under § 5 DDG and can be fined up to €50,000, and it is a frequent target of competitor Abmahnungen.

---

## 0. How to use this template

- Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`.
- Delete every block whose heading is marked **[conditional — keep only if …]** where the condition does not apply to CLARA.
- **Language:** This Impressum **must be provided in German** for the German/DACH audience. § 5 DDG is a German statute and the addressable audience is German-speaking; German courts expect the mandatory disclosures in German. An English translation may be offered additionally, but the German version is the legally controlling one. Keep the German block (Section 1) as the primary Impressum; the English mirror (Appendix A) is a convenience translation only.
- The Impressum must be reachable from **every page** with a link literally labelled **„Impressum"** (this exact word is the safe convention), no more than two clicks away, and readable without registration or JavaScript gymnicks.

> § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 DDG requires information enabling **rapid electronic contact and direct communication**, including an **e-mail address**. > Notes on trade specifics:
> - A plain **Kleingewerbe / Einzelunternehmen is not entered in the Handelsregister** → delete the Handelsregister lines; there is no HRA/HRB number to state.
> - Only if the founder registers as **e.K.** (or forms a UG/GmbH) is there a Handelsregister entry, which then **must** be disclosed under § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 DDG together with the register number.
> - If the activity is a **Gewerbe**, the Gewerbeanmeldung itself is **not** a § 5 DDG disclosure item (do not publish the Gewerbeschein), but the underlying classification (Freiberufler vs. Gewerbe) drives whether the Handelsregister/Rechtsform lines above apply. This is flagged for founder review — productised SaaS is likely a **Gewerbe**, not a freiberufliche Tätigkeit.
> - CLARA's LLM-based feedback triage is **not** a regulated/authorisation-requiring profession, so there is **no supervisory authority (Aufsichtsbehörde) line** under § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 DDG and **no regulated-profession block** (Kammer/Berufsbezeichnung under § 5 Abs. 1 Nr. 5 DDG). Delete those — they are only for lawyers, doctors, tax advisors, architects, etc.

### **[conditional — keep only if a Berufshaftpflicht / professional-liability insurance is in place]**

**Berufs-/Betriebshaftpflichtversicherung**
Name und Sitz des Versicherers: {{INSURER_NAME_AND_ADDRESS}}
Räumlicher Geltungsbereich: {{GEOGRAPHIC_SCOPE, e.g. „Europäische Union" / „weltweit ohne USA/Kanada"}}

> **Is a Berufshaftpflicht disclosure needed?** There is **no general obligation to *carry*** professional-liability insurance for a SaaS provider (unlike lawyers/tax advisors, for whom it is compulsory). **But** under **§ 2 Abs. 1 Nr. 11 Dienstleistungs-Informationspflichten-Verordnung (DL-InfoV)**, **if** the founder *has* a professional/business-liability policy, the **insurer's name and address and the geographic scope of cover must be disclosed** to the customer before contract conclusion. Because CLARA concludes contracts online, the safe place to give this is the Impressum. So: **carrying the insurance is optional; disclosing it is mandatory once you have it.** If there is no such policy, delete this whole block. (Recommendation for founder: a Betriebs-/Berufshaftpflicht and/or IT-/Vermögensschadenhaftpflicht is strongly advisable for a data processor — discuss with the lawyer/broker; then disclose it here.)

> § 18 Abs. 2 MStV requires a **named responsible person with a German address** for digital services with **journalistic-editorially designed content** (e.g. a blog, news, opinion, guides, a content-marketing magazine). If CLARA's site is purely a product/marketing site with **no** journalistic-editorial content, this line is **not strictly required** — but it is standard, harmless, and future-proofs a blog/resources section, so keeping it is recommended. For a solo founder the responsible person is normally the founder personally, at the same address as above.

### Verbraucherstreitbeilegung / Online-Streitbeilegung

Wir sind **nicht bereit und nicht verpflichtet**, an Streitbeilegungsverfahren vor einer Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle teilzunehmen.

> **Two things changed here — read carefully:**
> 1. **EU ODR platform: DO NOT include the old link anymore.** The former Art. 14 ODR-VO obligation to link to `ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr` has been **repealed by Regulation (EU) 2024/3228**. The EU Online Dispute Resolution platform **stopped accepting new complaints on 20 March 2025 and was shut down on 20 July 2025.** Since then the ODR link **must be removed** from the Impressum, AGB, and e-mail footers — keeping the dead link is now itself abmahnfähig. This template therefore **omits** the ODR link on purpose. (This corrects the older "include the ODR link" convention.)
> 2. **§ 36 VSBG statement.** A general information duty about willingness to use a **Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle** (consumer arbitration board) still exists under § 36 Verbraucherstreitbeilegungsgesetz (VSBG). A business with **≤ 10 employees** on 31 December of the prior year is **exempt from § 36 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 VSBG** — so a solo founder need not state a general willingness. **However**, since CLARA is **B2B** (Verbraucher = consumers; business customers are not Verbraucher), the VSBG largely does not bite at all. The negative statement above is nonetheless the safe, common default. **If CLARA ever sells to consumers, revisit this with the lawyer** (and if you have voluntarily committed to a specific Schlichtungsstelle, § 36 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 VSBG requires you to name it with its address and website).
