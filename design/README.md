## Your concept — the C that closes the loop
1. **`01` C + closing segment** — an amber arc sits in the C's mouth, a hair from snapping it shut. Most literal to "close the loop."
2. **`02` C + incoming piece** — a chevron points into the gap: the piece *arriving* to close it. Adds direction/motion.
3. **`03` C + pulse** — a single signal hovers at the opening, about to complete the circuit. The most minimal and the most favicon-friendly.
4. **`04` C + lifted wedge** — the missing wedge pulled out, ready to drop back in. Squared caps; sharper, more "engineered/governance."
5. **`05` C that curls in** — the tail loops back toward the core: the feedback feeding itself. Speaks to *Adapt* / continuous learning.

## Other directions — portraying the business
6. **`06` Orbit loop** — a closed loop, a live signal travelling it, an outcome at the core. Consistent with the mark already on the website.
7. **`07` Signal → outcome** — a warm signal rising into a cool, proven outcome. The whole thesis (and the C·L·A·R·A flow) in one sweep. Least literal as a "C."
8. **`08` The loop, proven** — a loop open where the signal enters, with the outcome verified (check) inside. Marries *close the loop* with CLARA's *prove it worked*.

## Quick read
- **Most literal to your brief:** `01`, then `02`.
- **Strongest tiny (favicon/app icon):** `03` and `06` — they stay legible at 16px.
- **Most expressive of the business:** `07` (signal→outcome) and `08` (loop + proof).
- A natural lockup is **mark + “CLARA” in Space Grotesk** (shown on the ink tiles).
