The mark

Five segments build the C — one per CLARA stage — and the amber piece closes the loop. It reads as a C and a loop at a glance, as five deliberate steps up close, and it carries the act of closing (the amber) as the hero. Petrol on light, teal on dark, single colour where needed.

Use it right

Do

Keep the amber piece for the close. Give it clearspace equal to the gap height. Petrol on light, teal on dark, the wordmark in Space Grotesk. One flat colour when amber can't reproduce.

Don't

Don't recolour the amber to a second brand hue, fill the C solid, add a gradient to the mark, stretch it, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
