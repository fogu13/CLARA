# Thesis review addendum, 4 September 2026

Second pass over `thesis/manuscript/` at commit `8de01ff`, extending `review-2026-09-02.md`. Method: a full citation-verification sweep (193 in-text citations, reported separately in `review-2026-09-04-citations.md`), six independent examiner lenses over the whole manuscript (design-science examiner, statistician, Responsible-AI and EU law, editor, code auditor, product strategist; 72 findings), a batched adversarial check of every finding by two skeptics, and a first-hand re-check by me of every high-severity item against the code and data. The verdict column below records both.

## What changed since 2 September

The first review understated the problem in three places.

1. **Chapter 4 describes the superseded build in far more places than DP4.** The `auto_execute` gate, the compliance checker and its regulatory knowledge base, PII masking before the model, pgvector retrieval of learnings, the learnings search interface and the rule-builder surfacing all exist only under `reference/elvis/` (the React/Supabase iteration). CLARA has none of them. Some of the replacements are stronger than what is described (every action requires an approval record), some weaker (raw feedback text reaches the model).
2. **The "LLM enrichment path" scored in §5A is not the artifact's enrichment stage.** The harness sends a four-line zero-shot prompt with a three-class sentiment set and a `risk` field; production uses a four-class set with `mixed`, an urgency rubric, tags, few-shot exemplars and an injection guard. Every RQ3a and RQ3b number describes a component that was never run, and the 0.86-versus-0.97 explanation in §5A.6 and §6.4 is therefore incomplete.
3. **Appendix B asserts legal statuses that cannot apply.** "Compliant" against Articles 12, 14, 15 and 17, which bind providers of high-risk systems only, while the thesis itself classifies the artifact as not high-risk.

Plus one arithmetic bug in the harness that made the routing results look worse than they are, and a pooled optimisation/held-out split that makes the golden-set results look better than they are.
