# Thesis review addendum, 4 September 2026

Second pass over `thesis/manuscript/` at commit `8de01ff`, extending `review-2026-09-02.md`.

Method: a citation-verification sweep over all 193 in-text citations (reported separately in `review-2026-09-04-citations.md`); six independent examiner lenses over the whole manuscript (design-science examiner, statistician, Responsible-AI and EU law, editor, code auditor, product strategist), 12 findings each; two adversarial skeptics per finding, one checking the manuscript text and one checking the underlying code, data or source; a completeness critic; and a first-hand re-check by me of every high-severity item. Disputed statistics are reproduced by `review-2026-09-04-recompute.py`, which runs from files already in the repository.

Of the 72 lens findings, **66 were confirmed** (58 by both skeptics; 8 by the evidence skeptic alone, where the text skeptic found the manuscript as quoted and deferred to the code or data), **5 were narrowed** (one skeptic disputed the framing while the other confirmed the underlying facts) **and 1 was refuted**, with a smaller defect surviving. The narrowed and refuted ones are reported below with what survives, because a review that only reports its hits is not a review. Three of my own 2 September conclusions and one from the first draft of this addendum were also wrong; they are corrected in Part C.

## What changed since 2 September

The first review understated the problem in three places.

1. **Chapter 4 describes the superseded build in far more places than DP4.** The `auto_execute` gate, the compliance checker and its regulatory knowledge base, the learnings search interface and the rule-builder surfacing exist only under `reference/elvis/` (the React/Supabase iteration); pgvector retrieval of learnings and PII masking before the model exist in neither build. CLARA has none of them. Some of the replacements are stronger than what is described (every action requires an approval record), some weaker (raw feedback text reaches the model).
2. **The "LLM enrichment path" scored in §5A is not the artifact's enrichment stage.** The harness sends a four-line zero-shot prompt with a three-class sentiment set and a `risk` field; production uses a four-class set with `mixed`, an urgency rubric, tags, few-shot exemplars and an injection guard. Every RQ3a and RQ3b number describes a component that was never run, and the 0.86-versus-0.97 explanation in §5A.6 and §6.4 is therefore incomplete.
3. **Appendix B asserts legal statuses that cannot apply.** "Compliant" against Articles 12, 14, 15 and 17, which bind providers of high-risk systems only, while the thesis itself classifies the artifact as not high-risk.

Plus one arithmetic bug in the harness that made the routing results look worse than they are, and a pooled optimisation/held-out split that makes the golden-set results look better than they are.
