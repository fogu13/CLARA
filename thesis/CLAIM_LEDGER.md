# Claim-to-evidence ledger (12 September 2026 review: R0–R4, C1–C4, E1, E2; 13 September: F, H; 17 September: I)

One row per claim touched by the review rounds since 12 September 2026. Appendix D remains
the traceability matrix by hypothesis and design principle; this ledger is the per-claim audit
of what evidence stands behind each sentence after the review edits, and is pointed to
from Appendix D. Line numbers are those of the manuscript files at the head of the 17 September
2026 revision (commit bcf7ee4 for `04_artifact.md`, 4dd3654 for the other chapters and
Appendices B, C and D, 3500165 for Appendix G). "@ commit" names the last commit on this
branch (`claude/relaxed-babbage-of6lum`) that touched the artefact, as `git log -1 -- <path>`
reports it, whether that commit is one of the review-response commits or an older one the
branch inherits; an artefact edited in the same pass as the ledger row is marked "this
commit".

**Evidence types** (kept distinct throughout): authored fixtures · AI-generated labels ·
second rating of unverified provenance · star-rating proxies · simulated outcomes · model
comparisons · real intervention observations · repository record · protocol. Repeat runs on
the same items are stable across repeated runs on the same items, not replication over new
items.

**Status vocabulary:** supported · narrowed (claim retained in a weaker form) · corrected ·
pending regeneration (evidence will be re-produced by a harness or code change) · pending human
collection (needs a person to rate, run, confirm or collect) · not conducted (the practitioner
study; contract of 17 September 2026, section 2). "Exploratory" appears below only as a
statistical qualifier of post-hoc comparisons, never as an evidence status.
