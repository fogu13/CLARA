# Recruitment outreach (working copy, not bound into the thesis)

Goal: book **12–15 interviews** (overbook to ~18). ICP: marketing / product / CX practitioners,
**2+ years' experience, companies of ~10–500 staff, that actively collect customer feedback.**
Where to find them: LinkedIn, communities (Demand Curve, CX Accelerator, r/marketing, r/analytics),
your own network + referrals from each interviewee.

> Replace `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[your form URL]`, `[scheduling link]` before sending.
> Personalize the first line of every message.

The participant information sheet and consent form that these messages point to are in
`consent_and_recruitment.md` (Appendix A). Send in small batches and track replies; aim for a mix
of roles and company sizes; ask every interviewee for 1–2 referrals at the end; confirm whether
OPIT permits a small incentive (e.g., a coffee voucher) and the findings summary as a thank-you.

---

## Outreach messages

### A1. LinkedIn connection note (≤300 chars)
> Hi [Name], I'm doing MSc research on how teams turn customer feedback into action (and where it
> stalls). Would value ~25 min of your real experience. No pitch, and I'll share what I find. Mind if
> I connect?

### A2. LinkedIn DM / InMail (after connecting)
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick context: for my MSc in Responsible AI I'm researching how
> marketing/product/CX teams *actually* act on customer feedback: what works, what stalls, and whether
> teams remember what worked. You've clearly lived this at [Company].
>
> Could I borrow **~25–30 candid minutes**? It's research, not a sales call; I just want your real
> experience, and I'll send you the anonymized findings. Full disclosure: the prototype we'll look at
> is one I built as part of this research, so critical reactions are exactly what I need. If a call's
> tricky, there's a **5-minute survey** instead: [your form URL].
>
> Would early next week work? Here's my calendar: [scheduling link].

### A3. Email
**Subject options:** "20 min for MSc research on customer feedback?" · "How does [Company] act on customer feedback?" · "Quick research favour (no pitch)"
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm an MSc (Responsible AI) student researching how teams turn customer feedback into action, and
> where the loop breaks. Given your work at [Company], your perspective would be really valuable.
>
> Would you be open to a **25–30 minute** video chat in the next week or two? It's purely research
> (no selling), it's anonymized, and I'll share the findings back. In fairness you should know the
> prototype we'd look at is one I built for this research; honest criticism helps me most. Prefer
> async? A **5-minute survey**: [your form URL].
>
> Grateful either way, thank you for considering it.
> Elvis Shehi · [scheduling link]

### A4. Referral ask (to your own network)
> Quick favour: I'm interviewing marketers / product / CX folks at ~10–500-person companies for my MSc
> on customer-feedback workflows. Know anyone who'd give me 25 candid minutes (or do a 5-min survey)?
> A one-line intro would mean a lot, and I'm happy to share the findings with you too.

### A5. Gentle follow-up (after ~5 days, no reply)
> No worries if now's hectic, [Name]. Even the **5-minute survey** would genuinely help: [your form URL].
> Thanks either way, and good luck with [something specific].
