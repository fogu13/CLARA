# Interview & Task-Session Guide (semi-structured)

**Study:** *Closing the Loop, Building the Memory*, MSc Responsible AI (OPIT).
**Duration:** ~25–30 min. **Format:** online, recorded with consent.
**Goal:** evidence on H1–H8 (current practice) + task-based reaction to the prototype (usefulness, usability, trust).

> Confirm consent (Appendix A) is signed before recording. Personalise the opening. Keep it a conversation, not a script; probes are prompts, not a checklist.

## 0. Warm-up (2 min)
- Your role, team, company size, and what customer feedback you work with day to day.

## 1. Current practice: how feedback becomes action (10 min) → H1, H2, H5, H8
- Walk me through what happens to a piece of customer feedback at [company], from arrival to (maybe) a change. *(probe each stage)*
- Where does it most often **stall**? *(probe: ownership, routing, prioritisation)*
- How do signals from different sources (surveys, tickets, reviews, analytics) come together, or don't they? → H2
- When you act on feedback, how do you know it **worked**? Do you measure closure? → H8
- A year later, does the team **remember** what worked? Where does that knowledge live?

## 2. Task-based prototype session (10–12 min) → RQ4, H4, H6, H7
Share the prototype. Ask the participant to think aloud. Capture task success + time-to-action (facilitator + `events` telemetry).
- **T1 (Triage):** here's a batch of signals; find the most urgent problem and explain why.
- **T2 (Govern an action):** decide who should own this theme and whether its actions need one approver or two (four-eyes). *(probe: is there any action here you would let run without a person approving it? why/why not?)* → H6, H7
- **T3 (Approve):** an action is waiting in the approval queue; decide and act. *(probe: is the audit trail enough to be accountable?)* → H7
- **T4 (Closure & memory):** find whether a past action actually resolved its problem, and what was learned. → H8

## 3. Reaction & trust (4 min) → RQ4
- What would make you trust this enough to use it on real customers?
- What's missing or would block adoption?
- (If relevant) How do the human-approval and audit features change your comfort with automation? → H6, H7
- Roughly how much of your week would the approval and audit steps cost, and is that a price you would pay? → RQ2 (friction)

## 4. Close (1 min)
- SUS + TAM forms (Appendix A). Anyone you'd introduce me to? Thank you; findings will be shared.

**Facilitator captures per session:** task success (T1–T4), observed time-to-action, notable quotes (anonymised), and any bug/usability friction.
