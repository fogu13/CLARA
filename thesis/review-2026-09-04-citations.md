# Citation verification report, 4 September 2026

Companion to `review-2026-09-02.md` and `review-2026-09-04-addendum.md`. Every in-text citation in the manuscript (193 distinct surname-year keys across Chapters 0–7 and Appendices B–D) was checked by an agent with web access against the reference list and the source, with the claim sentence(s) in hand. The environment blocks most publisher, DOI and index hosts and caps web searches, so agents often worked from indexed abstracts, mirrored BibTeX records and full-text mirrors; each entry names what was read. Where no adequate copy was reachable the verdict is *unverifiable*, never guessed. Adversarial refuters ran on 40 of the flagged items before the session limit stopped them: 24 upheld the problem, 16 could not access the source, none refuted.

|---|---:|

Reading the table: *partial* means the source is on the topic but the sentence attributes to it a number, mechanism or finding it does not contain, or overstates it. *Bibliographic entry wrong* covers wrong journal, wrong year, truncated title, authors elided to et al., or a different paper by the same first author. Most partials are fixed by one clause; the four unsupported ones need a different source or no source.
