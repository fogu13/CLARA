# Traceability Matrix: Hypotheses → Questions → Evidence

This appendix makes the chain from claim to evidence auditable, and states honestly what each claim's **status** is: *measured* (quantified against data), *demonstrated* (instantiated and shown in the artifact), *perception* (assessed via practitioner instruments), *pending* (instrument ready, data not yet collected), or *not evaluated*. It is the single place to see what the thesis proves versus what it argues; Chapter 1 (§1.7) carries its short form, and the claim-to-evidence ledger `thesis/CLAIM_LEDGER.md` lists, claim by claim with file and line, the evidence artefact, its commit, its evidence type and its status after the 12 September 2026 review. The working hypotheses H1–H8 live here and in the instruments' coding labels; the chapters answer the research questions.

## Reading

The **measured** rows concern triage quality (RQ3a) and escalation equity (RQ3b), a precondition of the loop, corroborated by two gold standards (§5A.3–5A.6 seed labels; §5A.7 in-repo golden set + star-proxy), neither of which is human-labelled: the seed labels were drafted by a language-model research assistant with the datasets and used as delivered (§3.7), and the golden set was authored in the repository with an AI coding assistant (§5A.7); the star rating is the one human-origin reference, and the blind second rating of §3.7 is the one human check on the risk labels (κ = 0.55 unweighted, 0.70 linear-weighted, n = 40; 38 of 40 within one level; seven of ten seed-critical items rated high; a second person's rating on the owner's attestation, with exposure to AI-generated suggestions unrecorded, `evaluation/results/PROVENANCE_kappa.md`). Since 5 September 2026 both streams score the same *pipeline* as well as the same *model*: the production enrichment stage with exemplars scores 0.83 sentiment on the seed-label corpus against 0.97 on the curated set, so what separates the two figures is the gold standard and the label scheme rather than the prompt or the exemplars, and the conservative figure is the one quoted (§5A.4.2). On severity the same stage scores 0.90 urgency on the curated set, which was authored to the production rubric, and 0.50 on the seed risk labels, which were not: the rubric sits one level below the seed labels, and the matrix records that as a measured, uneven capability. The in-repo corroboration has moved beyond its 60-case, 3 July 2026 state: the set grew to 80 items by authoring 20 German cases (adversarially verified by two independent skeptic agents; 19/20 confirmed, exactly one urgency dispute accepted), then to **100 items (72 en / 28 de)** with the German stratum completed to the two-tag rubric. The published snapshot (6 September 2026) reports sentiment **99%** (95% Wilson interval 0.95–1.00), urgency **91%** (0.84–0.95) and tag F1 (fuzzy) **78.7%** at *n* = 100, with the held-out half reported separately at 96.7% / 93.3% (*n* = 30; accuracies of the exemplars-on configuration on a validation split scored on every run, not a paired held-out exemplar effect); the 18 July snapshot it replaces read 97% / 90% / 82.7%. Per-language accuracies are published alongside their denominators; the DE/EN urgency gap, one point in July, is seven points in September (85.7% against 93.1%, *n* = 28 and 72). The configured production model scores 95% / 82% on the same set, unpublished. The designed inference is the **in-run paired A/B**, not the cross-run delta; the model is non-deterministic even at temperature 0: few-shot exemplars lift exact-tag-set agreement from 8.75% to 28.75% (McNemar *p* = 0.001), and at *n* = 100 the urgency effect reaches significance too (83%→90%, *p* = 0.039, in three same-day re-runs of the identical items, which bound model noise rather than item sampling; the exemplar set also changed between the two sample sizes) after being reported as underpowered and **not claimed** at *n* = 80 (*p* = 0.219); on 6 September it reads 78%→91% (*p* < 0.001) on GLM-5.2 and 82%→82% on the production model, so it is model-specific. Tag metrics are not comparable across the DE-rubric completion, which improved them partly by construction; disclosed in the published provenance. Two disclosures travel with these numbers wherever they appear: the German stratum is **authored**, not naturally occurring, and the hallucination heuristic is EN-scope only by construction. Of the **distinctive** claims, DP2's *influence* is now measured (remedy adoption, §5A.7) while its *benefit*, like DP1's, still awaits a live outcome contract on non-simulated data (Ch 6, §6.5, direction 3). H1 and H8 remain demonstrated and perception-tested. The matrix is the honest map of that boundary.
