# Traceability Matrix: Hypotheses → Questions → Evidence

This appendix makes the chain from claim to evidence auditable and states each claim's evidence status in the vocabulary of §1.7: *implemented and tested* (a mechanism exists in code and is covered by regression tests), *demonstrated* (instantiated and shown in the artifact), *measured (bounded)* (quantified against data, within stated limits), *descriptive* (differences reported without a test of significance or a ranking), *literature-supported motivation* (argued from the literature, not tested here), *not conducted* (the study that would supply the evidence was not run) and *untested hypothesis* (a design commitment whose benefit has not been examined). "Exploratory" is a statistical qualifier for the post-hoc comparisons of Chapter 5, not a status. Chapter 1 (§1.7) carries the short form of this matrix, and the claim-to-evidence ledger `thesis/CLAIM_LEDGER.md` lists, claim by claim with file and line, the evidence artefact, its commit and its status after each review round. The working hypotheses H1–H8 live here and in the instruments' coding labels; the chapters answer the research questions. Deployment claims are dated against the record of 15 September 2026 (§4.1; Appendix G.8): mechanisms implemented and regression-tested at commits from 2 September 2026 onward were not deployed at the time of writing.

## Reading

The measured (bounded) rows concern enrichment agreement (RQ3a), and the descriptive row concerns escalation differences across source strata (RQ3b); both are preconditions of the loop, not the contribution itself, and both rest on reference standards neither of which is human-labelled, with the second rating of the risk seeds reported as second-rating agreement with independent human provenance unverified (§3.7; Appendix G.1). The design principles are implemented and tested and demonstrated; whether a contracted measurement or a perishable memory improves decisions or customer outcomes is open, and DP2's decay is an untested hypothesis. Every practitioner-facing status is not conducted, because the study of §5B had not run at the time of writing. Mechanisms committed from 2 September 2026 onward were implemented and tested but not deployed, and the rows above say so where it matters.
