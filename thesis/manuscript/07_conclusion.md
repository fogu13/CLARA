# Chapter 7: Conclusion

This thesis set out to address a well-documented but stubborn problem: organisations collect abundant customer feedback, yet closing the loop on it remains the weakest practice in feedback management, and the communication of feedback to those who could act is too slow to drive action (Forrester, 2025b). The binding constraint is not collection or even analysis but **operationalisation under governance**: turning insight into action that is routed, executed, *measured*, and *remembered*. Framed as Design Science Research, the work designed, built, and evaluated a working platform that instantiates the full **Signal → Insight → Action → Learning** cycle, with Responsible-AI controls (human-in-the-loop approval, audit trails, transparency, and EU AI Act / GDPR alignment) embedded as properties of the loop rather than as a bolt-on.

The contributions answer the research questions in the order in which the evidence can carry them. What is **measured** answers RQ3a and RQ3b. A reproducible evaluation on **188 real customer signals from English- and German-language sources** (scored as English paraphrases; §3.7) showed that triage accuracy is strongly method-dependent. A rule-based lexicon floor is inadequate on paraphrased operational feedback (sentiment accuracy 0.37, risk macro-F1 0.26), a lightweight learned model reaches usable accuracy (sentiment 0.78, risk macro-F1 0.65), and a contextual LLM path on the artifact's model is the strongest predictor on both tasks (sentiment 0.86, risk macro-F1 0.68; the paired sentiment lead over the learned model is statistically significant, McNemar p = 0.029, though a single flipped item would put it at 0.043 or 0.052, while the risk lead is not, p = 0.644). That the contextual gain is large on sentiment and slim on severity is itself part of the answer: the loop's precondition is satisfiable by more than one method, and the choice among them is a design trade-off rather than a foregone conclusion. The sharpest consequence is not accuracy but escalation equity. The learned model escalates 62.5% of the genuinely critical German-source signals against 95.1% of the English-source ones, a gap that is statistically significant (Fisher p = 0.026), while the contextual path's difference between the strata is not detectable (87.5% against 87.8%, p = 1.0); the keyword floor escalates none of the eight German-source signals, but eight signals cannot separate that from chance. Method choice can determine whose problems reach a human. That is the answer to **RQ3b**, and it is indicative rather than established: the corpus confounds language with sector, the German-source gold-escalate stratum is eight signals, the strata are source-language strata of English paraphrases rather than German text, and a learned model trained on this corpus inherits its imbalance in a way a zero-shot model does not. A second, in-repo gold standard, hand-authored rather than independent, supplies convergent evidence: on the artifact's own committed 100-case bilingual golden set, the production enrichment stage reaches 97% sentiment and 90% urgency accuracy, with a statistically significant few-shot lift on exact tag-vocabulary agreement (McNemar p = 0.001) and, in three same-day re-runs, on urgency (83% → 90%, p = 0.039), a claim withheld at n = 80 and held as exploratory because the sample grew and the exemplar set changed between the two tests. That a generic prompt on the same model scores 0.86 against the independent star proxy while the production stage scores 0.97 against curated labels is itself instructive: prompt, exemplars, label scheme and gold standard all differ, and the conservative figure is the one this thesis quotes. Naive triage fails on real feedback, learned models are necessary, and the strongest method clears the accuracy bar the loop requires. What is **demonstrated** answers RQ1 and RQ2. The artifact shows that the loop can be closed by design: actions are bound to outcome contracts and measured for recurrence with a graded, refusing estimator, learnings are retained as perishable, retrievable memory, and every action is a draft whose approval is graduated by consequence class and recorded against a hashed evidence pack; the alignment with the EU AI Act and GDPR is set out as a design walkthrough that says which articles bind and which are adopted voluntarily (Appendix B). These commitments are abstracted into five transferable design principles and two implementation decisions (Chapter 6). What remains **exploratory** is RQ4: the practitioner study's protocol, instruments and fallback reporting rule are in place, and its results are reported at whatever sample it reaches, labelled as such (§5B).

The work also clarifies the artifact's position in a crowded market. Where leading feedback-intelligence platforms detect and route, this artifact's defensible distinction is **measured closure plus a decaying learning memory under explicit governance**, integrated with rather than competing against the tools organisations already use.

Its limitations are stated plainly. The quantitative corpus is modest and balanced rather than population-representative. Four of its six gold-labelled fields are scored, and two of those, journey stage and recommended owner, only under a supplied closed inventory, which makes their figures an upper bound on free-form production routing, while theme and recommended action carry labels but no metric at all. Language is confounded with sector in the corpus, the texts are English paraphrases of multilingual sources, and the cross-language claim is confined to escalation recall on a German-source stratum of eight signals. The qualitative study is small-N and establishes design validity, not causal effect. And the researcher is also the designer. These bound the claims to transferable design knowledge and a situated instantiation, which is exactly what Design Science Research offers, rather than to universal or commercial certainty.

The broader contribution is a demonstration that an action-taking AI system in customer operations can be **both useful and responsible by design**: closing the loop and governing the closing are not in opposition, but two halves of the same well-designed workflow. The same verify-before-believe standard the artifact imposes on its own actions was applied to the thesis's own claims, which is the only basis on which a researcher who is also the builder can ask to be believed; Appendix D records where that standard left each claim. Turning a measured loop into demonstrated, longitudinal business value, and extending the governance from design commitment to continuous, monitored practice, is the work that remains.
