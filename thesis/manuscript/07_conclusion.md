# Chapter 7: Conclusion

This thesis set out to address a well-documented but stubborn problem: organisations collect abundant customer feedback, yet fewer than a third systematically close the loop on it (Bone et al., 2017). The binding constraint is not collection or even analysis but **operationalisation under governance**: turning insight into action that is routed, executed, *measured*, and *remembered*. Framed as Design Science Research, the work designed, built, and evaluated a working platform that instantiates the full **Signal → Insight → Action → Learning** cycle, with Responsible-AI controls (human-in-the-loop approval, audit trails, transparency, and EU AI Act / GDPR alignment) embedded as properties of the loop rather than as a bolt-on.

The contributions answer the research questions as follows. For **RQ1** and **RQ2**, the artifact demonstrates that the loop can be closed by design: actions are bound to outcome contracts and verified for resolution, learnings are retained as perishable, retrievable memory, and autonomy is graduated and auditable. These commitments are abstracted into six transferable design principles (Chapter 6). For **RQ3a**, a reproducible evaluation on **188 real customer signals from English- and German-language sources** (scored as English paraphrases; §3.7) showed that triage accuracy is strongly method-dependent. A rule-based lexicon floor is inadequate on paraphrased operational feedback (sentiment accuracy 0.37, risk macro-F1 0.26), a lightweight learned model reaches usable accuracy (sentiment 0.78, risk macro-F1 0.65), and the artifact's contextual LLM path is the strongest predictor on both tasks (sentiment 0.86, risk 0.72; the paired sentiment lead over the learned model is statistically significant, McNemar p = 0.029, while the risk lead is not, p = 0.644). That the contextual gain is large on sentiment and slim on severity is itself part of the answer: the loop's precondition is satisfiable by more than one method, and the choice among them is a design trade-off rather than a foregone conclusion. The sharpest consequence is not accuracy but escalation equity. The keyword floor escalates none of the eight genuinely critical German-source signals while catching a fifth of the English-source ones, the learned model escalates 62.5% against 95.1%, and only the contextual path reaches near-parity (87.5% against 87.8%). Method choice determines whose problems reach a human. That is the answer to **RQ3b**, bounded by a corpus in which language is confounded with sector, the German-source gold-escalate stratum is only eight signals, and the strata are source-language strata of English paraphrases rather than German text. A second, independent gold standard supplies convergent evidence: on the artifact's own committed 100-case bilingual golden set, it reaches 97% sentiment and 90% urgency accuracy under the production configuration, with a statistically significant few-shot lift on both exact tag-vocabulary agreement (McNemar p = 0.001) and urgency (83% → 90%, p = 0.039, three consecutive runs), a claim withheld at n = 80, where the same test returned p = 0.219, and made only once the sample could carry it. Its limits (the authored German stratum, the English-scope hallucination check, and German tag metrics that improved partly by construction when the gold was completed) are disclosed in the model card. That the same pipeline scores 0.86 against the independent star proxy and 0.97 against curated labels is itself instructive: the conservative figure is the one this thesis quotes. This is a substantive result. Naive triage fails on real feedback, learned models are necessary, and the strongest method clears the accuracy bar the loop requires. For **RQ4**, a complete qualitative study (protocol, instruments, and analysis plan) is in place to assess practitioner usefulness, usability, and trust, with results to follow from the interview data.

The work also clarifies the artifact's position in a crowded market. Where leading feedback-intelligence platforms detect and route, this artifact's defensible distinction is **measured closure plus a decaying learning memory under explicit governance**, integrated with rather than competing against the tools organisations already use.

Its limitations are stated plainly. The quantitative corpus is modest and balanced rather than population-representative. Four of its six gold-labelled fields are scored, and two of those, journey stage and recommended owner, only under a supplied closed inventory, which makes their figures an upper bound on free-form production routing, while theme and recommended action carry labels but no metric at all. Language is confounded with sector in the corpus, the texts are English paraphrases of multilingual sources, and the cross-language claim is confined to escalation recall on a German-source stratum of eight signals. The qualitative study is small-N and establishes design validity, not causal effect. And the researcher is also the designer. These bound the claims to transferable design knowledge and a situated instantiation, which is exactly what Design Science Research offers, rather than to universal or commercial certainty.

The broader contribution is a demonstration that an action-taking AI system in customer operations can be **both useful and responsible by design**: closing the loop and governing the closing are not in opposition, but two halves of the same well-designed workflow. The discipline that produced it is worth naming last. An unplanned external review was verified claim by claim rather than believed (§5A.8). A fairness comparison the corpus could not support was retracted rather than defended (§5A.5). An exemplar effect was withheld while underpowered and claimed only when the sample could carry it (§5A.7). And an evaluation script that reported success on an empty result was treated as a finding about the harness rather than quietly rerun. In each case the verify-before-believe standard the artifact imposes on its own actions was applied to the thesis's own claims, which is the only basis on which a researcher who is also the builder can ask to be believed. Turning a measured loop into demonstrated, longitudinal business value, and extending the governance from design commitment to continuous, monitored practice, is the work that remains.
