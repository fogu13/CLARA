# Chapter 7: Conclusion

This thesis set out to address a well-documented but stubborn problem: organisations collect abundant customer feedback, yet closing the loop on it remains the weakest practice in feedback management, and the communication of feedback to those who could act is too slow to drive action (Forrester, 2025b). The binding constraint is not collection or even analysis but **operationalisation under governance**: turning insight into action that is routed, executed, *measured*, and *remembered*. Framed as Design Science Research, the work designed, built, and evaluated a working platform that instantiates the full **Signal → Insight → Action → Learning** cycle, with Responsible-AI controls (human-in-the-loop approval, audit trails, transparency, and EU AI Act / GDPR alignment) embedded as properties of the loop rather than as a bolt-on.

The contributions answer the research questions as follows. For **RQ1** and **RQ2**, the artifact demonstrates that the loop can be closed by design: actions are bound to outcome contracts and measured for recurrence, learnings are retained as perishable, retrievable memory, and autonomy is graduated and auditable. These commitments are abstracted into six transferable design principles (Chapter 6). For **RQ3a**, a reproducible evaluation on **188 real customer signals from English- and German-language sources** (scored as English paraphrases; §3.7) showed that triage accuracy is strongly method-dependent. A rule-based lexicon floor is inadequate on paraphrased operational feedback (sentiment accuracy 0.37, risk macro-F1 0.26), a lightweight learned model reaches usable accuracy (sentiment 0.78, risk macro-F1 0.65), and a contextual LLM path on the artifact's model is the strongest predictor on both tasks (sentiment 0.86, risk macro-F1 0.68; the paired sentiment lead over the learned model is statistically significant, McNemar p = 0.029, though a single flipped item would put it at 0.043 or 0.052, while the risk lead is not, p = 0.644). That the contextual gain is large on sentiment and slim on severity is itself part of the answer: the loop's precondition is satisfiable by more than one method, and the choice among them is a design trade-off rather than a foregone conclusion. The sharpest consequence is not accuracy but escalation equity. The learned model escalates 62.5% of the genuinely critical German-source signals against 95.1% of the English-source ones, a gap that is statistically significant (Fisher p = 0.026), while the contextual path's difference between the strata is not detectable (87.5% against 87.8%, p = 1.0); the keyword floor escalates none of the eight German-source signals, but eight signals cannot separate that from chance. Method choice determines whose problems reach a human. That is the answer to **RQ3b**, bounded by a corpus in which language is confounded with sector, the German-source gold-escalate stratum is only eight signals, and the strata are source-language strata of English paraphrases rather than German text. A second, in-repo gold standard, hand-authored rather than independent, supplies convergent evidence: on the artifact's own committed 100-case bilingual golden set, the production enrichment stage reaches 97% sentiment and 90% urgency accuracy, with a statistically significant few-shot lift on exact tag-vocabulary agreement (McNemar p = 0.001) and, in three same-day re-runs, on urgency (83% → 90%, p = 0.039), a claim withheld at n = 80, where the same test returned p = 0.219, and held as exploratory because the sample grew and the exemplar set changed between the two tests. Its limits (the authored German stratum, the English-scope hallucination check, and German tag metrics that improved partly by construction when the gold was completed) are disclosed in the model card. That a generic prompt on the same model scores 0.86 against the independent star proxy while the production stage scores 0.97 against curated labels is itself instructive: prompt, exemplars, label scheme and gold standard all differ, and the conservative figure is the one this thesis quotes. This is a substantive result. Naive triage fails on real feedback, learned models are necessary, and the strongest method clears the accuracy bar the loop requires. For **RQ4**, a complete qualitative study (protocol, instruments, and analysis plan) is in place to assess practitioner usefulness, usability, and trust, with results to follow from the interview data.

The work also clarifies the artifact's position in a crowded market. Where leading feedback-intelligence platforms detect and route, this artifact's defensible distinction is **measured closure plus a decaying learning memory under explicit governance**, integrated with rather than competing against the tools organisations already use.

Its limitations are stated plainly. The quantitative corpus is modest and balanced rather than population-representative. Four of its six gold-labelled fields are scored, and two of those, journey stage and recommended owner, only under a supplied closed inventory, which makes their figures an upper bound on free-form production routing, while theme and recommended action carry labels but no metric at all. Language is confounded with sector in the corpus, the texts are English paraphrases of multilingual sources, and the cross-language claim is confined to escalation recall on a German-source stratum of eight signals. The qualitative study is small-N and establishes design validity, not causal effect. And the researcher is also the designer. These bound the claims to transferable design knowledge and a situated instantiation, which is exactly what Design Science Research offers, rather than to universal or commercial certainty.

The broader contribution is a demonstration that an action-taking AI system in customer operations can be **both useful and responsible by design**: closing the loop and governing the closing are not in opposition, but two halves of the same well-designed workflow. The discipline that produced it is worth naming last. An unplanned external review was verified claim by claim rather than believed (§5A.8). A fairness comparison the corpus could not support was retracted rather than defended (§5A.5). An exemplar effect was withheld while underpowered and claimed only when the sample could carry it (§5A.7). And an evaluation script that reported success on an empty result was treated as a finding about the harness rather than quietly rerun. In each case the verify-before-believe standard the artifact imposes on its own actions was applied to the thesis's own claims, which is the only basis on which a researcher who is also the builder can ask to be believed. Turning a measured loop into demonstrated, longitudinal business value, and extending the governance from design commitment to continuous, monitored practice, is the work that remains.
