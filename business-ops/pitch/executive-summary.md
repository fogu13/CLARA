# CLARA — Executive Summary

**The EU-sovereign, governed, self-improving engine for Voice of Customer.**

CLARA turns raw customer feedback — app-store reviews, support tickets, surveys — into governed, auditable, action-ready insights, and learns from the business outcomes those actions produce. Built EU-first, local-first, GDPR- and EU AI Act-ready by design.

---

## The Problem

Product and CX teams are drowning in unstructured feedback across a dozen channels. The incumbent Voice-of-Customer tools that promise to fix this are largely **US-based and not EU-sovereign** — a non-starter for the DACH mid-market, EU marketplaces, and regulated fintech/insurance buyers who cannot or will not send customer data to US clouds. Meanwhile, most "AI insight" tools stop at a dashboard: they surface themes but never close the loop to prove which actions actually moved the business.

## The Solution — and why it's different

CLARA runs one governed pipeline: **ingest → enrich (sentiment, urgency, snake_case theme tags) → cluster into themes → synthesize auditable, action-ready insights → measure outcomes and learn.** Three things make it defensible:

- **EU-sovereign & governance-first.** Data residency in the EU, local-first architecture (no US clouds required), GDPR + EU AI Act built in and fully auditable. Governance is the *wedge*, not overhead.
- **Outcome-grounded self-improvement.** CLARA closes the loop — Signal → Insight → Action → measured Outcome → Learning — so it compounds and gets more accurate with use. Defensible and hard to copy.
- **Industry-adaptable with zero fine-tuning.** Generates industry-specific tag vocabularies with zero config — no frontier-model fine-tuning. That keeps inference COGS low and gross margins at **89–94%**.

## Real Validation (proof, not vapor)

Tested on **real scraped datasets across three industries** — Henkel (B2B adhesives), Lieferando (food delivery), Trade Republic (fintech):

- **~90% sentiment accuracy** vs. star-rating proxy; urgency calibration valid across all three industries.
- Industry-specific tag vocabularies generated **with zero configuration**.
- Real, actionable themes surfaced — e.g. Trade Republic *"pervasive support unresponsiveness on critical financial issues"* (14 signals clustered); Lieferando *"refunds denied for undelivered orders"* (28 signals clustered).

Originated as a **master thesis** with academic rigor: honest eval harness, bootstrap confidence intervals, adversarially-verified test set.
