**1. You're drowning in feedback and still guessing.**

"The AI said so" doesn't survive an audit or a board question. CLARA makes every insight governed and auditable, traces it to the underlying signals, and closes the loop by measuring the business outcome after you act.

## How it works — 3 steps

**1. Ingest & enrich.** Connect your feedback sources (app-store, reviews, support, surveys). CLARA enriches every signal with sentiment, urgency, and concise `snake_case` theme tags — using an industry-specific vocabulary it generates for you with zero configuration and no fine-tuning.

**2. Cluster & synthesize.** CLARA clusters signals into real themes and synthesizes governed, action-ready insights — each one traceable to the exact signals behind it, so it stands up to scrutiny.

**3. Act & learn.** You act on the insight. CLARA measures the outcome and feeds it back: **Signal → Insight → Action → measured Outcome → Learning.** The engine compounds — it gets sharper on your business over time.
