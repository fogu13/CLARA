## Regulated DACH fintech / neobanks / insurtech / insurance

### N26 — ★★★★★  ·  _Germany (Berlin)_
*BaFin-licensed mobile neobank operating across the EU with millions of retail current-account customers.*

- **Why a fit:** Very high public review volume, BaFin/ECB-regulated bank handling EU personal financial data (residency and GDPR critical), clear budget. Textbook CLARA buyer.
- **Observable pain:** Trustpilot n26.com shows ~475+ pages of reviews (thousands). Dominant recurring complaint themes: sudden account blocks/freezes with no explanation, no phone hotline for free accounts (support only via in-app chat rated ~4/10), slow GDPR requests, funds stuck, account closures. Polarised distribution masks a large angry cohort.
- **What CLARA solves:** CLARA clusters the account-blocking / KYC-freeze / support-unreachability complaint themes across app-store + Trustpilot + support into governed, auditable, EU-resident insight so CX and Compliance can prioritise fixes and evidence remediation to BaFin.
- **Approach angle:** Lead with 'you have thousands of public account-freeze complaints and BaFin visibility — CLARA turns that noise into governed, EU-resident, auditable theme insight without exporting customer data to US tools.'
- **Target roles:** Head of Customer Experience, VP Customer Operations, Chief Compliance Officer, Head of Customer Insights, VP Product
- **Contact pathway:** Public imprint: https://n26.com/en-eu/imprint ; support via in-app chat / help center. Approach via LinkedIn.
- **Sources:** https://www.trustpilot.com/review/n26.com · https://germanpedia.com/n26-bank-review-germany/ · https://marcthiele.com/notes/stay-away-from-n26

### Trade Republic — ★★★★★  ·  _Germany (Berlin)_
*BaFin-licensed neobroker/bank; commission-free investing, savings and cards for millions of EU retail investors.*

- **Why a fit:** Extreme public feedback volume, regulated bank, EU-residency-sensitive financial data, strong budget. This is the anchor archetype the segment is modeled on.
- **Observable pain:** Trustpilot ~3.3/5 across 33,000-35,500+ reviews; ~1 in 5 give 1 star. Recurring themes: bot-loop / template support responses, no phone support, multi-day waits, unresolved transfer failures, KYC verification blocks, tax-document errors. Clear structured-complaint corpus.
- **What CLARA solves:** CLARA ingests the tens of thousands of reviews + support tickets, surfaces the 'bot loop / transfer stuck / tax doc error' themes with urgency and sentiment, and produces governed action-ready insight to route to Ops and Product.
- **Approach angle:** '35k Trustpilot reviews and a 1-in-5 one-star rate is a data goldmine you can't legally pump into a US LLM — CLARA gives you EU-sovereign theme synthesis and an outcome loop.'
- **Target roles:** Head of Customer Care, VP Operations, Chief Customer Officer, Head of Product Insights, Head of Compliance
- **Contact pathway:** Public imprint: https://traderepublic.com/imprint ; in-app support / contact form. Approach via LinkedIn.
- **Sources:** https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.traderepublic.com · https://www.newtrading.io/trade-republic-review/ · https://curvo.eu/article/trade-republic-review
