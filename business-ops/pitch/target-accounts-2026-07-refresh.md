## Market tailwinds (verified, cite freely)

- **BaFin DORA-AI guidance** (announced 18 Dec 2025; EN version 30 Jan 2026): AI systems classified as
  network/information systems under DORA — AI-powered VoC tooling at supervised firms must sit inside a
  DORA-compliant ICT-risk framework, human-in-the-loop recommended. Structurally favors CLARA's governed,
  auditable design. *Caveats: non-binding Orientierungshilfe; EU data residency is our positioning, not a
  DORA mandate — don't overclaim.*
- **BaFin complaint statistics 2025** (Fachartikel 20 Apr 2026): securities complaints 5,910 → 6,860
  (+16%); arbitration applications +~35% to 1,521, of which 767 (50.4%) neo-brokerage; "Erreichbarkeit
  oder Kundenservice" named as a very frequent subject.
- **Energy**: Schlichtungsstelle Energie 2025 report (1 Feb 2026): 19,074 applications across ~719
  companies; ~1/3 concentrated on just four supplier groups (unnamed).
- **Telco**: BNetzA 2025 report: arbitration applications 5,524 — more than double the prior all-time
  record (2,534); provider participation-refusal up to 30%.
- **Sovereignty market**: Deutsche Telekom × NVIDIA "Industrial AI Cloud" Munich (~€1B, announced
  4 Nov 2025, live early 2026), explicitly branded "AI sovereignty for Germany and Europe" — validates the
  EU-sovereign differentiator at market level (and makes DT itself a telco candidate).
