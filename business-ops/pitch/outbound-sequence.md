# CLARA — Founder Outbound Sequence

A ready-to-use, founder-led outbound playbook to book first discovery calls with ICP buyers. Combines cold email + LinkedIn across **6 touches over 14 days**. Every template is copy-pasteable with `{{PLACEHOLDERS}}`. German and English cold-email versions are both provided.

**Who this is for:** the solo technical founder running outbound personally (no SDR). Keep it high-signal, low-volume: **15–25 hand-picked prospects per week**, not a spray-and-pray blast. Founder-led, specific, honest — EU-sovereignty and governance as the through-line.

---

## 0. Before you send: positioning cheat-sheet

Keep every message consistent with this. Do not invent competing facts.

**One-liner:** CLARA is an EU-sovereign, governed, self-improving Voice-of-Customer engine. It turns raw customer feedback (app-store, reviews, support, surveys) into governed, auditable, action-ready insights — without sending a single row of customer data to a US cloud.

**The wedge (say this out loud):** Governance is the feature, not the overhead. EU data residency + local-first, GDPR + EU AI Act built-in, fully auditable.

**Proof you can name (real, already done):** Tested on real scraped datasets across 3 industries — Henkel (B2B adhesives), Lieferando (food delivery), Trade Republic (fintech). ~90% sentiment accuracy vs star-rating proxy; urgency calibration valid across all 3; industry-specific tag vocabularies generated with **zero config / zero fine-tuning**. Real themes surfaced, e.g. Trade Republic *"pervasive support unresponsiveness on critical financial issues"* (14 signals clustered) and Lieferando *"refunds denied for undelivered orders"* (28 signals clustered).

**Origin credibility:** Started as a master's thesis with academic rigor — honest eval harness, bootstrap confidence intervals, adversarially-verified test set.

**The ask:** a 20-minute call, or a paid design-partner pilot (one metric, weekly syncs, testimonial on success).

**ICP priority order:**
1. DACH mid-market B2B SaaS product/CX teams
2. EU e-commerce / marketplaces
3. Regulated DACH fintech / insurance (highest willingness-to-pay)

**Titles to target:** VP/Head/Director of Customer Experience, Head of Product, Head of Support/Customer Operations, Chief Product Officer; in regulated segments also add Head of Compliance / DPO as a secondary influencer.

**Anchor pricing (only if asked):** Growth tier **1,990 EUR/mo** (~24k/yr, up to ~50k signals/mo) is the land. Starter **690 EUR/mo**; Enterprise **5,000+ EUR/mo** custom with on-prem/local deployment. Annual prepay ~17% off. Do **not** claim "cheaper than the incumbents" — the closest competitor's entry is reportedly ~$12k/yr (review-site estimate); CLARA matches entry economics and wins on governed outcomes + EU sovereignty, not on price. Lead with the pilot, not the price.

## 1. Sequence at a glance

| Touch | Day | Channel | Goal | Rule |
|------|-----|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Day 0 | LinkedIn — connection request (no pitch) | Get on their radar | Personalized note < 300 chars |
| 2 | Day 1 | Cold email #1 (primary) | Earn a reply / the call | One idea, one CTA |
| 3 | Day 4 | LinkedIn — soft touch (comment or DM) | Warm the thread | Reference their content, no ask |
| 4 | Day 6 | Cold email #2 — follow-up (new angle) | Re-surface with proof | Add the validation, not "just bumping" |
| 5 | Day 10 | LinkedIn DM (if connected) OR email #3 | Direct, low-friction ask | Offer a 1-page teardown |
| 6 | Day 14 | Cold email #4 — breakup | Close the loop, leave door open | Permission-to-close, keep it warm |

**Cadence hygiene**
- Never pitch inside the LinkedIn connection request.
- Emails 1–4 live on the **same thread** (reply to your own message) so context travels.
- If they reply at any point, **stop the sequence** and go human.
- Send Tue–Thu, 07:30–09:00 CET lands best for DACH operators.
- Track per prospect: sent, opened, replied, call-booked. Kill what doesn't reply after touch 6.

## 10. Discovery-call script (20 minutes)

Goal of the call is **not** to close — it's to qualify and land a **paid design-partner pilot** (one metric, weekly syncs, testimonial on success). Talk 30%, listen 70%.

**Minute 0–2 — Frame**
> "Thanks for the time, {{FIRST_NAME}}. Plan for the 20 minutes: I'll ask a few questions about how {{COMPANY}} handles customer feedback today, show you 2–3 minutes of CLARA on real data, and if it's a fit we talk about a small pilot. If it's not, I'll say so. Fair?"

**Minute 2–8 — Discover (dig, don't pitch)**
- "Where does customer feedback land for you today — app reviews, support tickets, surveys, all of it?"
- "Who actually reads it, and what do they do with it? Walk me through the last time feedback changed a decision."
- "How long from 'a pattern is emerging' to someone acting on it? Days? Weeks?"
- "When Legal or your DPO looks at a feedback/analytics tool, what do they ask for?" *(listen for data residency, DSGVO, subprocessors — this is your wedge)*
- "Have you tried tooling for this? What made you stop, or what's missing?"
- "Were you quoted by Enterpret or a similar tool? What was the quote?" *(validates real competitor entry pricing in-market — review-site data suggests ~$12k/yr entry, but treat it as unverified until you hear actual quotes)*
- "If you could reliably answer one question from your feedback every week, what would it be?"

**Minute 8–13 — Show (tie to what they just said)**
> "Let me show you what I mean. This is CLARA on a real dataset. Notice three things: it tagged sentiment at ~90% accuracy with no fine-tuning; it generated this tag vocabulary itself — I didn't configure it; and it clustered these {{N}} separate complaints into one action-ready theme, with a full audit trail of why. Same thing you described with {{THEIR_PAIN}} — this is where it'd surface."

**Minute 13–17 — The pilot ask**
> "Here's what I'd suggest. A paid design-partner pilot: we pick **one metric that matters to you** — say, time-to-insight on {{FEEDBACK_SOURCE}}, or urgent-issue detection rate. We run CLARA on your real data, sync weekly for {{4–6}} weeks, and if it delivers, you give me a testimonial and we talk about continuing. Low commitment, real data, one clear success measure. Would that be worth doing?"

**Handling the big objections**
- *"Is our data safe / where does it run?"* → "That's the whole point of CLARA. EU data residency, local-first — it can run on-prem for the pilot. Nothing goes to a US cloud. I'll send the DPA, TOMs and subprocessor list so your DPO can review before we touch any data."
- *"We already have a tool / do this in-house."* → "Makes sense — most teams do it partially. The gap I usually find is the audit trail and the EU-sovereignty story when Legal gets involved. Where does your current setup leave you exposed there?"
- *"What does it cost?"* → "The pilot is scoped and small so you can prove value first. If we continue, most teams land on the Growth plan at 1,990 EUR/mo for up to ~50k signals a month; there's a Starter at 690 and an Enterprise tier with on-prem for regulated setups. But let's prove the metric before we talk numbers."
- *"Send me some info."* → "Happy to — I'll send a one-pager. But the fastest way to know if this is real is to let me run CLARA on your own feedback. Can you point me at a source, and I'll bring the results to a 20-minute follow-up?"
- *"Not right now."* → "Understood. When's the right moment to revisit — next quarter, after {{EVENT}}? I'll set a reminder and come back with fresh results relevant to {{COMPANY}}."

**Minute 17–20 — Close to a next step (never end open)**
> "Two ways forward: I run CLARA on a public feedback source of yours and send a one-page teardown by {{DATE}}, or — if you're already interested — we scope the pilot and I send a short proposal. Which is easier for you?"

Then, always: confirm the next step in writing, put the calendar hold in during the call, and send the recap email the same day.

## 12. Operating notes for the founder

- **Volume:** 15–25 net-new prospects/week is plenty solo. Depth beats breadth — every message should prove you looked at *their* company.
- **Personalize the first line, not the whole email.** The `{{FEEDBACK_SOURCE}}` and `{{TOPIC_OR_PRODUCT}}` fields are where you earn the read.
- **Lead with the pilot, not the price.** You're recruiting design partners, not selling seats yet.
- **Be honest about the proof.** Henkel/Lieferando/Trade Republic are *validation datasets*, not customers. Say so. It builds trust and it's the CLARA through-line.
- **Segment your angle:** compliance-first for fintech/insurance (Variant A), speed/zero-config for product teams (Variant C), cost for ops/finance (Variant B).
- **Stop-on-reply.** The sequence exists to start a conversation, not to complete all six touches. The moment they engage, go fully human.
- **Measure it like the product measures itself:** log reply rate and call-booked rate per subject line and per segment, then double down on what converts — the same Signal → Insight → Action → Outcome loop CLARA runs.
