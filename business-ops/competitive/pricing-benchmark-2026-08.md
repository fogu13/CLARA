## Recommendation (implemented on the dark /pricing page)

1. **Publish, when the pilot motion can absorb calls.** The gate stays until Elvis flips it; content is ready.
2. **One displayed price per tier** (was: "from €490 / typically €690" — two numbers per tier invites "what does €490 buy?" with no answer). Display the anchors: **Starter €690/mo · Growth €1,990/mo (Most popular) · Enterprise from €5,000/mo**, all "billed annually"; one footnote: monthly billing available at a higher rate; annual prepay ≈ 2 months free. The €490 floor stays an internal negotiation floor, not a public promise.
3. **Keep the pilot as a first-class product:** "Governed pilot — 6 weeks, €3–5k, one success metric, pre-negotiated conversion." It is the actual land motion and belongs on the page as its own card, not a footnote.
4. **A visible "Most popular" label** on Growth (was border-color only).
5. **No named competitor comparisons on the public page** — comparative advertising is pending the Fachanwalt review (see teardowns README / UWG §7). The category fact we *can* state neutrally: "Published pricing — rare in this category." Named numbers stay in this memo for sales conversations.
6. **Overage stays qualitative on the page** ("metered per 1,000 signals; heavy usage upgrades a tier"). Publishing a €/1k rate is a commitment; the model's €8–15/1k range is not yet validated. Decide after the first pilots.
