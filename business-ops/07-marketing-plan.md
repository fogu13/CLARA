# CLARA — 12-Month Marketing Plan

*EU-sovereign, governed, self-improving customer-feedback intelligence.*

> **Scope & honesty note.** This is a solo-founder, founder-led marketing plan for the first 12 months post-launch. It is deliberately lean: one operator, a small budget, and channels that compound rather than channels that require a team or paid-media spend to work. Every number below is either drawn from the CLARA company brief (pricing, ICPs, validation results) or is a stated *marketing target* — a hypothesis to be measured and revised, not a forecast.

---

## 1. Positioning & Messaging House

### 1.1 Core promise

**CLARA turns raw customer feedback into governed, auditable, action-ready insight — without sending a single review to a US cloud.**

For EU teams that live with GDPR and the EU AI Act, CLARA is the Voice-of-Customer engine that is sovereign by default, auditable by design, and gets sharper every quarter because it learns from the business outcomes its insights produce.

**One-liner (elevator):**
> CLARA is the EU-sovereign Voice-of-Customer engine: it ingests your app-store, review, support and survey feedback, clusters it into themes, and produces governed, auditable insights you can act on — and it self-improves from measured outcomes, with no US cloud and no fine-tuning.

**Category:** Voice-of-Customer / customer-feedback analytics / CX-insight software — reframed around **EU sovereignty + governance + self-improvement** as the axis of differentiation, because the incumbent category is US-based and neither sovereign nor governance-first — price is no longer the gap; governance is.

### 1.3 Pillar messaging detail

**Pillar 1 — EU-sovereignty & local-first**
- *Claim:* Your customer data never has to leave the EU. Local-first architecture, no US hyperscaler dependency, on-prem/local deployment available at the Enterprise tier.
- *Why it matters:* DACH mid-market, EU marketplaces, and regulated fintech/insurance increasingly cannot or will not send customer text to US clouds. Sovereignty is a buying criterion, not a nice-to-have.
- *Proof:* Local-first, no-fine-tune architecture; on-prem option; low inference COGS → 89–94% gross margins (we can price sovereignty in without pricing customers out).

**Pillar 2 — Governance & auditability**
- *Claim:* Every insight traces back to the exact signals that produced it. GDPR-processor posture, ready AVV/DPA + TOMs + subprocessor list + DPIA support pack; EU AI Act limited/minimal-risk classification (text triage, not biometric).
- *Why it matters:* Buyers in regulated segments must show their work to auditors, DPOs and boards. Governance de-risks the purchase and shortens legal review.
- *Proof:* Auditable insight lineage; Art. 50 transparency handled ahead of its 2 Aug 2026 applicability.

**Pillar 3 — Outcome-grounded self-improvement**
- *Claim:* CLARA closes the loop — Signal → Insight → Action → **measured Outcome** → Learning — so its recommendations get more accurate and more valuable the longer you use it.
- *Why it matters:* Most VoC tools produce dashboards that go stale. CLARA compounds, which is both a retention driver and a defensible moat.
- *Proof:* The self-improvement loop is architectural, not a slide; industry-adaptable with **zero fine-tuning** of frontier models.

### 1.4 Messaging guardrails (what we never say)
- We do **not** claim to replace analysts; we make them 10× faster.
- We do **not** invent metrics. ~90% sentiment accuracy is *vs a star-rating proxy*; we say so.
- We do **not** name-drop Henkel / Lieferando / Trade Republic as customers — they are **validation datasets** (public/scraped), and we describe them exactly that way.
- We do **not** promise the ~800k EUR ARR ramp as fact — it is an illustrative hypothesis.

## 2. Brand & Voice

**Brand personality:** The rigorous European operator. Calm, exact, evidence-first. Think "senior analyst who reads the regulation and ships the product," not "growth-hack SaaS."

**Voice principles:**
1. **Specific over grand.** Numbers, named datasets, real themes — never "revolutionary AI."
2. **Honest by default.** State the caveat before the reader finds it. Honesty is a differentiator in an AI market full of vapor.
3. **Sovereignty & governance as the through-line.** Almost every asset connects back to "your data, your rules, in the EU."
4. **Compounding, not hype.** We talk about learning loops and measured outcomes, not magic.
5. **Bilingual-aware.** English as the primary content language for reach; German for high-intent DACH assets (landing pages, compliance one-pagers, key LinkedIn posts) where trust and precision matter most.

**Visual/verbal identity cues:** clean, technical, document-like; diagrams over stock photos; the Signal → Insight → Action → Outcome → Learning loop as a recurring visual motif; a compliance "trust strip" (GDPR · EU AI Act · EU data residency · DPA-ready) reused across site and decks.

**Naming conventions:** snake_case theme tags shown in product screenshots (they *are* the product's fingerprint); "governed insight," "insight lineage," "outcome-grounded loop" as owned phrases.

## 3. Target Segments & Buyer Personas

Priority order follows the brief's ICPs. Willingness-to-pay rises down the list; sales cycle length rises with it too.

### ICP 1 — DACH mid-market B2B SaaS (product / CX teams) — *primary land*
- **Land tier:** Growth (1,990 EUR/mo, up to ~50k signals/mo).
- **Personas:** VP/Head of Product; Head of Customer Experience / Support Ops; Product Analyst.
- **Pains:** Feedback scattered across app stores, Zendesk, surveys, sales calls; manual tagging doesn't scale; "we know customers are unhappy but can't prioritize"; leadership wants themes tied to revenue/churn.
- **Triggers:** Missed a churn spike they should have seen; a bad app-store review cycle; new Head of Product wants a VoC system in 90 days; annual planning needs a ranked problem list.
- **Message that lands:** "Ranked, evidence-backed themes with lineage — in days, not a quarter of manual tagging."

### ICP 2 — EU e-commerce / marketplaces
- **Land tier:** Growth, expanding toward Enterprise at high signal volumes.
- **Personas:** Head of CX / Customer Operations; Marketplace Trust & Safety lead; Growth/Retention lead.
- **Pains:** Enormous review + support volume; recurring operational failures buried in the noise (cf. Lieferando "refunds denied for undelivered orders," 28 signals); need to move from anecdote to quantified issue fast.
- **Triggers:** Rising refund/complaint rates; a PR-sensitive review trend; peak-season support overload.
- **Message that lands:** "Surface the operational failures that are quietly costing you refunds and reputation — quantified and auditable."

### ICP 3 — Regulated DACH fintech / insurance — *highest willingness-to-pay, expand target*
- **Land tier:** Enterprise (5,000+ EUR/mo, on-prem/local deployment).
- **Personas:** Head of CX / Complaints; Chief Compliance / DPO; Head of Product in a regulated line.
- **Pains:** Cannot send customer data to US clouds; complaint handling is regulated; support failures on financial matters are material risk (cf. Trade Republic "pervasive support unresponsiveness on critical financial issues," 14 signals); auditors demand traceability.
- **Triggers:** Regulatory review; a complaints-handling audit; EU AI Act readiness push ahead of Aug 2026; a data-residency requirement from InfoSec.
- **Message that lands:** "Sovereign, on-prem VoC with insight lineage your auditors and DPO will accept."

## 4. Channel Strategy

Selected for a solo founder: high-leverage, compounding, low-cash. Ranked by expected contribution.

### 4.1 Thesis-backed thought-leadership content (flagship)
The master thesis is an unfair advantage: honest eval harness, bootstrap CIs, adversarial test set, real cross-industry results. This becomes a body of "proof content" no competitor can cheaply copy.
- **Formats:** a public "validation report" / technical write-up; per-industry deep dives (adhesives B2B, food delivery, fintech); an "AI-Act-ready VoC" explainer; methodology posts (how sentiment accuracy was measured *honestly*).
- **Role in mix:** the top-of-funnel credibility engine and the SEO cornerstone.

### 4.2 LinkedIn — founder-led (primary distribution)
- Solo founder in Berlin posting 3×/week: build-in-public, EU-sovereignty POV, validation findings, governance explainers, teardown of a public review dataset (with tasteful, dataset-not-customer framing).
- Goal: become a recognizable EU voice on "governed, sovereign VoC / customer-feedback AI." Engagement → DMs → discovery calls → pilots.

### 4.3 SEO / organic
- Own long-tail, high-intent, low-competition EU terms: "EU-sovereign customer feedback analysis," "GDPR-compliant Voice of Customer," "EU AI Act text analytics," "customer feedback analytics on-premise," plus German equivalents ("DSGVO-konforme Kundenfeedback-Analyse," "Voice of Customer DSGVO").
- Cornerstone pages per pillar + per ICP; the validation report as the highest-authority magnet.

### 4.4 Communities
- Where the ICPs actually are: DACH product/CX Slack & communities (Product-led, Support Driven, CX/Support ops groups), EU-founder communities, GDPR/DPO and EU-AI-Act practitioner forums, relevant subreddits.
- Contribution-first (answer questions, share the validation methodology), never spammy.

### 4.5 Partnerships & integrations
- **Integration wedge:** Zendesk / Intercom / Freshdesk (support), app-store review sources, survey tools (Typeform, etc.). Each integration = a distribution surface and a reason-to-buy.
- **Referral partners:** EU-focused DPO consultancies, fractional CX consultants, boutique CX agencies serving DACH mid-market; Berlin startup ecosystem (EXIST/IBB networks).
- Co-marketing: joint webinar / guest post with one compliance or CX partner per quarter.

### 4.6 Selective events
- Not a booth strategy. Attend/speak at 2–4 targeted EU events/year (DACH product, CX, and data-sovereignty/AI-governance meetups; Berlin startup events tied to non-dilutive funding networks).
- Aim: a speaking slot on "governed, sovereign VoC" > passive attendance.

## 8. Marketing KPIs

Tracked monthly; targets are hypotheses to validate, not commitments. North-star bridges marketing to the GTM reality that **pilots convert deals**.

**North-star metric:** **Qualified pilot conversations booked per month** (discovery calls with an in-ICP buyer that reach pilot discussion).

### Funnel KPIs
| Stage | Metric | 12-month target (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | LinkedIn followers (founder) | 1,500 → 5,000 |
| Awareness | Monthly organic site visitors | 300 → 2,500 |
| Awareness | Validation-report downloads | 300 cumulative |
| Interest | Newsletter subscribers | 750 |
| Interest | SEO cornerstone pages ranking pg.1 (long-tail) | 6+ |
| Consideration | Discovery calls booked | 6–10 / month by Q4 |
| Decision | **Paid pilots started** | 4–8 across the year |
| Advocacy | Published case studies / testimonials | 2 |

### Efficiency & quality KPIs
- **Content cadence adherence** (LinkedIn 3×/wk, 1 cornerstone/mo, biweekly newsletter) — leading indicator; the one metric fully in the founder's control.
- **Discovery-call → pilot rate** and **pilot → paying (Growth/Enterprise) rate.**
- **CAC (fully-loaded incl. founder time proxy)** vs LTV — must stay comfortably inside 89–94% gross margins.
- **Channel attribution of pilots** (which channel sourced each pilot) — reallocate quarterly toward what sources pilots.
- **Time-to-first-value in pilots** (days to first ranked, lineage-backed theme) — both a product and a marketing proof point.

### Reporting rhythm
- **Weekly:** cadence adherence, calls booked, pilot pipeline.
- **Monthly:** full funnel dashboard + channel attribution; kill/scale decisions on experiments.
- **Quarterly:** review targets vs actuals, refresh the content calendar, and re-rank channels by *pilots sourced* — the only vanity-proof output that matters.
