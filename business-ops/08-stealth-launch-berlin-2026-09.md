## 0. Honest reset

The July 90-day plan (5 pilots by day 60) shows **zero completed items** in `90-day-tracker.md` at what would be week 8 — the summer went into the product, the evals, the science review, and the thesis. That was not wasted: it built exactly the assets a *proof-led, stealth* launch needs (measured metrics, calibration data, legal drafts, a verified 20-account list with warm paths). But the plan must now match demonstrated capacity: **solo founder, thesis due September, ~50% founder time until it is submitted.**

**Redefinition of "launch":** a signed, paid design partner — not a public announcement. Public visibility is deliberately deferred to **CCW Berlin, 23–25 Feb 2027 (Estrel)**, with a documented outcome case study in hand. Until then: no press, no Product Hunt, no launch posts, no logo wall. Quiet pilots, personal outreach, controlled case-study rights.

**Why stealth is right for CLARA (from the challenge analysis):**
1. German buyers punish overclaiming; CLARA's honest-metrics story needs α values and a selective-prediction page before loud claims (challenge #2).
2. "Close the loop" language is being commoditized by Enterpret/Dovetail/Qualtrics *right now* — arriving loudly with the same words is noise; arriving quietly with a **proven outcome** is category-defining (challenge #1).
3. The learning-memory moat is empty until pilots run — visibility before proof advertises the gap (challenge #5).
4. Competitors watching: Chattermill already took GetYourGuide (see `target-accounts-2026-07-refresh.md` downgrade).

## 2. What changes vs. the July plan (delta log)

1. **Pilot target: 2–3 paid, zero free.** (July: 2 free + 3 paid.) Free design partners under-commit and German buyers don't value free; a token price (€500–990/mo or €3–5k/90 days, credited to year one) qualifies seriousness. Cap the ambition at what one person can service weekly.
2. **Positioning lead: Beweisbarkeit.** Open every conversation and the demo with the governed action → outcome contract → "we prove whether it worked", not the feed/insights (the feed is the consolidation-graveyard category: zenloop→saas.group, Mopinion→Netigate, Usabilla→discontinued). Evidence for the pain: XM Institute Q1 2026 — only 1–2% of feedback gets follow-up; Forrester 2025 — only ~half can link CX metrics to outcomes.
3. **Accuracy claims: retire "97%".** Human-human sentiment agreement is ~80%; urgency IAA α≈0.63 in a 2026 benchmark. Publish instead: per-language metrics with CIs, Krippendorff's α from a human-audit sample, and a coverage-accuracy (selective-prediction) view; extend abstention beyond journey-stage to all enrichment fields. "Human-level agreement, audited, honest abstention" is the DACH-credible claim.
4. **Outbound legality tightened (verified 2026-08-29):** cold **email** requires prior express consent (§7 Abs. 2 Nr. 3 UWG) — and **cold LinkedIn/XING DMs are legally the same** (OLG Hamm, 03.05.2023, 18 U 154/22; applied by lower courts through 2025). The July sequence's "short DE email first touch" is out. Legal first touches: **warm reintroductions to genuine existing contacts (personal, non-advertising), research interviews, events, physical letters (opt-out only), and qualified cold calls with documented presumed consent (§7 Abs. 2 Nr. 1)**. The warm-path map (Deliverable 3) is now the *only* LinkedIn instrument — bridges, not cold DMs.
5. **Content: 36 posts → ~4–6.** One personal-voice post/week max, insight-led (complaint-statistics angles), never product-launch language. Stealth + capacity.
6. **Model-strategy musts folded in** (from the model doc): pin dated model IDs + record actual serving model in audit; retire the OpenCode-Zen preset's Google embeddings (EU-story leak); per-workspace AI config **before pilot #2** (tenant isolation); embed-at-ingest + Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B swap behind the qualification gate; laptop Ollama demo as the sovereignty showpiece in every meeting ("runs on this MacBook — your data never left the room").
7. **Grant reality (verified):** Berliner Startup Stipendium requires a team of ≥2 — **solo founders ineligible**; EXIST needs a German host university + pre-formation status — park unless a host materializes. **File GründungsBONUS Plus instead** (up to €50k, 50% of eligible costs, year-round, budget-limited — IBB Business Team; re-confirm portal status) and **Coaching BONUS** (50–100% subsidized coaching days). KfW StartGeld (now up to €200k, Dec 2025 change) is the debt backstop, not a first resort.
