# MVP Scope

## Positioning

The first sellable product is not a general customer intelligence platform.

It is:

> An evidence-backed action queue that helps teams decide what to do about recurring customer problems and prove whether the action worked.

## Initial Buyer

Start with teams that already feel the pain of converting messy feedback into accountable work:

- CX operations
- Product operations
- Support leadership
- Customer success leadership

Marketing and compliance become expansion stakeholders once intervention design and policy checks are reliable.

## Beachhead Customer

Best first segment:

- DACH or EU mid-market B2B SaaS and digital-service companies
- 50 to 500 employees
- Zendesk or Intercom for support
- Jira for delivery
- HubSpot or Salesforce for customer/account context
- Some product-event data, even if exported as CSV

This segment is mature enough to have connected systems, but not so complex that every deployment becomes a custom enterprise data program.

## First Workflow

1. Feedback and support tickets are imported.
2. Customer and account context is attached.
3. The system identifies recurring problems and evidence.
4. A problem appears in the Action Queue.
5. The system proposes:
   - a structural product or service action;
   - an immediate customer recovery action;
   - an optional audience or journey intervention brief;
   - an outcome contract.
6. A human owner approves, edits, or rejects the proposal.
7. The platform records the decision and later checks whether the expected outcome improved.

## Deliberately Out Of Scope

- Survey creation
- Full campaign delivery
- Full CDP functionality
- Autonomous customer contact
- Full causal attribution
- Full journey orchestration
- Generic chat-first analytics

## Success Metrics For Pilot

- Time saved in feedback triage
- Percentage of action proposals accepted or edited
- Reviewer trust score
- Evidence quality score
- Reduction in repeat complaints for accepted problem classes
- Improvement in one journey metric, where event data exists
